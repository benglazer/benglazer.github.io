How to play

  1. Collect the stars, then find the flag. You need every star to finish the maze. Take your time—there’s no timer.
  2. Move your way. Use the arrow keys, WASD, the on-screen arrows, or swipe on the board.
  3. Took a wrong turn? Undo takes back your last move for 1 coin. It won’t return used shop items or remove their score penalties. You can undo until you finish the maze.
  4. Need a little help? Buy supplies in the Shop, then use them when you need them. A hint shows up to ten moves to follow, chalk marks a spot you want to remember, and a lantern temporarily helps you see farther.
  5. Go for your best score. Fewer moves earn more points, up to 10,000. Each hint you use cuts your score by 20%, and each lantern by 10%. Chalk doesn’t affect your score.
  6. Finish mazes to earn coins. Spend them on supplies or a new look for your explorer. Higher scores earn more coins, and you can replay levels to earn more.
  7. Try another challenge whenever you like. Choose New maze for a different layout, or pick another level. After finishing a maze, choose Restart level to play it again for free. Starting over won’t return supplies you’ve already used.
  8. Come back for more. Your coins, unused supplies, purchases, and best scores save in this browser. The maze itself starts fresh when you reopen the game.
